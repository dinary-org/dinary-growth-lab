## 3. Audiovisual analysis: own render (`full_av`)

Rights basis: our own production, with all assets generated in the pipeline.

| Feature | Measured | Storyboard ground truth |
|---|---|---|
| Duration | 24.0 s (video stream; container 24.1 s from AAC padding) | 24.0 s |
| Edits detected (threshold 0.08) | 9 at 2.4, 5.0, 7.4, 9.8, 12.0, 14.6, 17.6, 20.4, 22.6 s | 9 at the same times |
| Mean shot length | 2.4 s | 2.4 s |
| Hard cuts (threshold 0.3) | 5 | — |
| First on-screen text / first speech | 0.0 s / 0.08 s | 0.0 / 0.08 |
| Speech ratio | 0.81 | — |
| Integrated loudness / true peak | −14.3 LUFS / −1.4 dBFS | target −14 / ≤ −1.0 |
| First↔last frame similarity (loopability) | 0.969 | designed loop |

**Analyzer calibration finding.** The common scene-change threshold of 0.3 found only 5 of 9
edits. It misses clock-to-clock changes where only the digits change on a similar gradient. 0.08
recovered 9/9 with no false positives; 0.05 added one false positive from the card animation.
Rhythm features now use 0.08, and hard cuts are still reported separately. Text-driven formats,
which are common in short-form, would otherwise look slower-paced than they are.

**Known OCR limits:** Tesseract returned nothing for the white rounded card at 19.0 s. It reads
very large clock digits as separated characters ("0 7 0 0"), and it misread one caption
("오심이분" for "오십이 분"). For text measurements, prefer caption sidecars when they exist.

The interpretation (hook mechanism, tension, loop incentive) is stored separately from these
measurements in the analysis JSON. Every claim cites a frame or timestamp.

## 4. Emergent clustering: mechanism demo (SYNTHETIC)

Corpus: random-syllable vocabularies (meaningless strings, so the corpus contains no taxonomy)
with hidden topic labels T1–T4 and format profiles F1–F3. There were two runs with a moving
30-day window. T3 ages out of the window; T4 appears only in the second window.

| Run | Space | Method | ARI vs hidden topic | ARI vs hidden format | Lineage |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | topic | HDBSCAN | **1.00** | 0.02 | 3 emerged |
| 1 | format | HDBSCAN | 0.04 | **0.76** | over-split (4 clusters for 3 profiles) |
| 2 | topic | HDBSCAN | **1.00** | 0.00 | T1, T2 persisted; T4 **emerged**; T3 **dissolved** |
| 2 | format | HDBSCAN | 0.02 | **0.69** | F1+F3 **merged** into one cluster |

Reading: topic recovery and lineage behave as designed, and "what" stays independent of "how".
The format space is the weaker one: it over-split in run 1 and under-split in run 2. Before
trusting format clusters on real data, validate them against a hand-labelled sample of about 50
analysed videos.

## 8. Sampling biases to expect once data flows

- `mostPopular` is a survivorship sample of already-viral items in the KR region.
- `search.list` with `order=viewCount` favours older items inside the time window, and search
  results are personalised and de-duplicated by YouTube.
- Shorts are inferred from duration (≤ 180 s), so non-Shorts uploads under 3 minutes get in.
- Shares, saves and retention for other creators' videos are never public. Shareability is
  therefore a proxy (comments and likes).
- Apify actors sample hashtag pages, which favour hashtag-using creators. Like counts may be
  hidden.
- Media-level (`full_av`) analysis is possible only for authorized media. Most competitor
  observations will be `metadata_only`, and format clustering on them is limited to form metadata.

### 9.2 Biases found in the real data, and what was changed

1. **Ad-served distribution.** The highest raw view counts were brand and public-agency
   campaigns: NORDISK ×4 (up to 10.8M), Samsung, SK telecom, 우리은행 ×2, HIRA ×2, 바이슬림. They
   have like/view ≈ 0.0000 against a corpus median of 0.0087. *Heuristic* flag: ≥ 100k views and a
   like rate below 10% of the corpus median. 13 videos were flagged; 2 are news channels (JTBC, MBN)
   and may be false positives, because news Shorts draw few likes. Flagged videos stay in the
   corpus but count as **zero** evidence of organic demand.
2. **The ads had already steered discovery.** Round 2's exploit terms `#경량패딩` and `#akmu`
   were learned from the NORDISK campaign (a light-padding campaign featuring an AKMU member). Ad
   suspects are now excluded from term learning.
3. **Query echo.** With adaptive search, many clusters are mostly the output of one query: 경량패딩
   89% from `#경량패딩`, "two/number" 94% from the probe `2`, AKMU clusters 100% from `#akmu`. Each
   cluster now reports `query_concentration`. When it is ≥ 0.6, saturation is treated as unknown and
   the opportunity carries a "query echo" caveat.
4. **Selection toward outliers.** `search.list order=viewCount` returns each creator's best
   videos, so the corpus median is 3.0× creator baseline. Creator-relative figures should be read
   *relative to each other*, not as typical performance.
5. **The `mostPopular` chart is mostly long-form.** Only 17 of 200 chart items were ≤ 180 s, so
   chart sampling contributes little to Shorts discovery.

### 9.3 Pipeline bugs found by the live run (all fixed, with tests)

| Bug | Effect | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Random probes could repeat on a cold start | 200 of 666 units spent on duplicate queries | sample without replacement |
| One channel's uploads playlist returned 404 | aborted all creator baselines | per-channel degradation, reason recorded |
| Re-discovery overwrote `first_seen_at` | would corrupt novelty / emergence timing | `upsert_video` keeps the first sighting; 117 rows repaired from snapshots |
| Unmeasured baselines stored as `n=0` | backfill skipped 158 creators | `None` = unknown, `0–4` = measured-but-too-few |
| Sync wrote child tables before parents | Supabase FK violation (rows kept queued, nothing lost) | parent-first table order |
| Outperformance scale saturated at 10× | top clusters tied on the main component | rescaled to saturate at 100× |

There are 32 topic clusters plus 143 unclustered videos. The ones with **independent** repetition
(several creators, low query concentration, no ad suspects) are the most informative:

| Cluster | Videos / creators | Median vs creator baseline | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `t30` 아빠 · 따뜻한 (hidden-motive warm reversals) | 7 / 7 | **12×** | titles withhold the reason (…이유, 정체, 가족사) |
| `t29` 반전드라마 · 시어머니 (twist family dramas) | 8 / 8 | **9.8×** | every member 78–178 s; #시니어드라마; several AI-produced channels |

Format clusters (5) can only use metadata here (duration, title shape, hashtags). They separate
"question titles", "number titles", and "long + short title" forms, but they are **not**
audiovisual format findings.

**Measured finding that contradicts the secondary evidence:** vendor guides cited 20–40 s as
typical for viral Shorts. In `t29` the outperformers run 78–178 s (median ≈ 146 s). Across the
whole corpus the median is 39 s.

The intervals overlap heavily, so **the order within the top four is not statistically

- The ranking has no cluster-coherence component. `t31` is a grab-bag of loosely related titles.
- Single-creator clusters can still reach an exploit slot through the one-per-topic diversity rule.

Both are on the next-iteration list.

### 9.6 Concepts from measured evidence

- **삼각김밥 두 개** (`c_20261031_hidden_kindness`, from `t30` and `t29`). A habit is misread,
  then a withheld reason reveals hidden kindness. It is fiction, labelled on screen as fiction:
  several cluster members present unverified stories as true, and we deliberately don't.
  **Rendered:** `outputs/videos/c_20261031_hidden_kindness.mp4`, 46 s, 15/15 QC checks.
  Frame review also caught a line-break orphan that QC cannot detect. Hypotheses include an A/B
  test of 46 s vs 120 s, because the measured cluster runs long.
- **우리 동네 사장님 어록** (`c_20261031_merchant_lines`, from `t1`). A consenting local merchant's
  quotable lines as a ranked countdown with a visit payoff. It is not rendered because it needs
  filming. The main hypothesis is explicitly at risk, since the effect may belong to one person.
